Basic

Two points: one at (x, y) and one at (y, x). Drag one and the other mirrors it.

Rings

Points arranged in two concentric circles using trigonometry. All controlled by radius and angle parameters.

Lines with affects

Three lines with different affects constraints controlling which endpoints can move.

Dragon Curve

A recursive dragon curve. Drag the endpoints or the curve to change its squareness.

Fractal Tree

A recursive tree. Drag branches to change the split angle and attenuation.
